% 1.5 System Limitations / Constraints
% --------------------------------

While well designed, the mobile application Lets Go Ride Sharing App has a number of limits and restrictions that need to be recognized for realistic expectations of the project.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{User Participation is essential for the success of ride matching:} A critical player of the process is having a large number of active users. There are fewer options passengers will have to match up with drivers during the first deployment because there is limited use of the system, and ecient ride matching is difficult.

    \item \textbf{Relying on Third-Party Services:} The app relies on third-party mapping APIs to provide location tracking, route calculation, and LLM APIs to enhance the chatbot feature. This could cause a period of downtime, API changes, or limitations on API rates from these third-party providers, and make the functionality of the application operate differently for some period of time, depending on the specific changes made.

    \item \textbf{Internet Connectivity \& Location Accuracy:} The need for real-time features such as ride-matching, location tracking and fare calculations come with the condition of robust Internet connectivity and accurate GPS information. Depending on the location, if GPS signal is not convenient or accurate, the user could experience poor performance and/or not have access to core features.

    \item \textbf{Comprehensive manual Security and Verification Overheads:} Manual validation of User documents (CNIC, driving license, vehicle registration) increases security but could slow down the Registration process. However, there may be delays for new users to leverage their full capacity with the platform, potentially impacting user acquisition and retention.

    \item \textbf{Automated Payment Gateways:} Payment Gateways are also outside of the scope of the current work. Transactions are dependent on cash payments and manual verification of bank payments and can lead to delays in payment confirmation and extra administration in reconciling payments.

    \item \textbf{Geographic Scope:} Early deployment to certain local communities and urban areas: During the initial stages, the application may be provided only for rural areas and for smaller cities, which will confine its scope of service and its usage and reduced accessibility.

    \item \textbf{Resource and Time Constraints:} Some high level features or deep testing of scalability testing could be planned as future improvements due to time and resources constraints as part of the academic project with a fixed timeline. A development team of two students reduces both the capacity for development and level of specialization.
\end{itemize}
